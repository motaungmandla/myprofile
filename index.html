About the Developer

Building AI for Sesotho speakers.

I'm Mandla Motaung, a final-year BSc Mathematics & Computer Science student at the National University of Lesotho. My work sits at the intersection of mobile engineering, machine learning, and African language technology.

I've published academic research on the SeSoDa dataset — a Sesotho dataset for AI research — and I'm actively developing a Sesotho Automatic Speech Recognition system to make AI inclusive for our nation.
